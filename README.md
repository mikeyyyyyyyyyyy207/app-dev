# My Favorite Movie
 
## Spider-Man: No Way Home (2021)
 **Spider-Man: No Way Home** is my favorite superhero movie. It follows Peter Parker,
 a young superhero whose identity is revealed to the world. He asks Doctor Strange for help,
 but the spell goes wrong and brings villains from other universes into his world.
 
### Why I Like It
- The action scenes are amazing and exciting.
- Spider-Man is brave and always tries to save people.
- The story teaches the importance of friendship and sacrifice.
 
### My Favorite Character
My favorite character is **Peter Parker / Spider-Man** because he is brave, kind, 
and never gives up even when things become difficult.
 
### What I Learned
The movie taught me that every decision has consequences
and that being a hero means making sacrifices to protect 
the people we love.
